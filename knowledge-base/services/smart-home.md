# Smart Home

## Overview

Installation and configuration of smart thermostats, video doorbells, cameras, smart locks, leak sensors, and whole-home packages. We install what we service: everything is set up on your accounts and phone before we leave, not left in a box of hubs. No monthly monitoring contracts required — you own the hardware.

## Process & timeline

Most installs are half-day visits. We confirm your Wi-Fi can support the devices (weak Wi-Fi is the #1 cause of "smart home junk drawer" abandonment), install, pair to your accounts, and walk you through the app before leaving.

## FAQs

**Which smart thermostat works with my HVAC?** Heat pumps, dual-fuel, and multi-stage systems need correct wiring/config or you lose efficiency (or heat). Our installers are HVAC-literate — that's the reason to not DIY this one; see the hvac service line for system questions.

**Do smart locks make my home less secure?** Reputable models are as pick-resistant as decent deadbolts and remove key-under-the-mat behavior. We disable default codes and set up guest access properly.

**What happens when the internet goes down?** Thermostats keep their schedule, locks work by keypad/key, cameras with local storage keep recording. We prefer devices with sane offline behavior and will say which ones those are.

**Are leak sensors really worth it?** If you travel, own a rental, or have a finished basement: yes, unambiguously. The shutoff valve turns a flood into a puddle. Insurance discounts of 5–10% are common — ask your carrier.
