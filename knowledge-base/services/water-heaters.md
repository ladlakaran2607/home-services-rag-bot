# Water Heaters

## Overview

Repair and replacement of tank, tankless, and heat-pump water heaters. Same-day replacement available on standard tank units when booked before noon (trucks carry 40- and 50-gallon units). No-hot-water calls are treated as priority, not emergency-fee, during standard hours.

Replacement quotes given by phone within a range, confirmed exactly on site before work. Standard tanks: 2–3 hours. Tankless conversions: half to full day (venting + gas line). Permit and inspection included; expansion tank and pan brought to code on every install.

## FAQs

**Repair or replace?** Tanks: under 6 years old and repairable, repair. Over 8–10 years (Denver's hard water is unkind), replacement is usually the honest answer, especially if the tank itself leaks — a leaking tank body is not repairable.

**Is tankless worth it?** Endless hot water, ~$100–$200/year energy savings, 20-year life vs 10–12 for tanks, but 2–3× upfront. Best fit: gas homes, families that run out of hot water, and long-stay owners. We'll do the math for your usage rather than push the upsell.

**What size tank do I need?** 40 gal suits 2–3 people; 50 gal for 3–5; large families or soaker tubs may need 75 gal or tankless. Recovery rate matters as much as size for gas units.

**Why is my water heater making popping noises?** Sediment buildup boiling water under the scale layer. A flush may fix it early; a heavily scaled old tank is on borrowed time.
