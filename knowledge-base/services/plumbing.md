## FAQs

**My water bill spiked — what should I check?** Silent toilet leaks are the #1 cause. Drop food coloring in the tank; if it reaches the bowl without flushing, the flapper leaks. We can also run electronic leak detection for hidden slab or wall leaks.

**Do I have polybutylene or galvanized pipe, and does it matter?** Homes built pre-1960 (galvanized) or 1978–1995 (poly-b) may have failure-prone piping. Both raise insurance premiums; we quote repipes with photos of what's actually in your walls.

**What water pressure is normal?** 40–80 PSI. Above 80 voids many fixture warranties and stresses the system; a failing PRV is the usual cause.

**Can you fix a leak under a slab?** Yes — options are spot repair (jackhammer access), rerouting the line overhead, or full repipe. We price all viable options.
