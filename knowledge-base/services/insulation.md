# Insulation & Air Sealing

## Overview

Attic insulation, air sealing, crawl space encapsulation, and wall insulation. The unglamorous work with the best payback in the trade: most Denver-area homes built before 2000 are under-insulated for our climate zone (5B), and air sealing before insulating is the step cheap contractors skip.

## FAQs

**What R-value do I need?** Colorado climate zone 5B: R-49 to R-60 in the attic. If you can see your ceiling joists above the insulation, you're roughly R-19 or less and losing meaningful money.

**Why air seal first?** Insulation slows heat conduction but does nothing for air leaking through gaps. Sealing first can cut air leakage 15–30% and makes the insulation perform at its rated value. Insulating over leaks is buying a sweater full of holes.

**Will this really lower my bills?** Typical combined attic package saves 10–25% on heating/cooling. Pairs directly with HVAC: a tighter home often needs a smaller (cheaper) system at replacement time — tell your HVAC estimator the insulation is planned.

**Is my old vermiculite insulation dangerous?** Possibly (asbestos risk in pre-1990 vermiculite). We don't disturb it; we refer licensed abatement first and insulate after clearance.
