# Roofing

## Overview

Roof repair, full replacement, and hail/storm restoration for asphalt, metal, and flat roofs. Denver's hail corridor is our specialty: we handle insurance claims end-to-end, including adjuster meetings and supplement documentation. Free inspections with photo reports.

## Services & pricing

- Roof inspection: free, with a photo/video condition report.
- Repairs (missing shingles, flashing, pipe boots, small leaks): $400–$1,800.
- Asphalt shingle replacement (architectural): $9,000–$18,000 for typical 2,000–3,000 sq ft homes.
- Class 4 impact-resistant shingle upgrade: adds $1,500–$3,500; most Colorado insurers discount premiums 10–30% for it.
- Metal roof (standing seam): $18,000–$35,000.
- Flat/low-slope (TPO/EPDM): $8–$14 per sq ft.
- Emergency tarping after storm damage: $400–$800, credited toward repair or claim work.

## Process & timeline

Repairs: quoted from inspection, usually done within a week. Replacements: most are one day of tear-off and install with a 2–4 week scheduling lead (longer in post-hail season). Insurance claims: we inspect first, tell you honestly whether damage will meet your deductible, and if so meet the adjuster on site. Workmanship warranty on replacement is 10 years, our longest.

## FAQs

**How do I know if I have hail damage?** From the ground you often can't. Granule piles at downspouts, dented gutters or AC fins are indicators. Our free inspection documents everything with photos before you ever call your insurer.

**Will a claim raise my rates?** Weather claims in Colorado are typically rated across a region, not individually, but multiple claims can affect you. If damage is marginal we'll tell you to wait.

**Repair or replace?** Under 30% of the roof affected and shingles still manufactured: repair. Widespread granule loss or brittle shingles: replacement math usually wins. We show you both numbers.

**Do you handle the insurance paperwork?** We document, meet the adjuster, and submit supplements for missed items. You pay your deductible — by Colorado law (SB 38) we cannot waive it.
