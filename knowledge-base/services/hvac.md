# HVAC — Heating & Cooling

## Overview

Full-service heating and cooling: furnace and AC repair, replacement, new installs, heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, and seasonal maintenance. NATE-certified technicians, all major brands serviced (Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Mitsubishi). No-heat and no-cool calls qualify for 24/7 emergency dispatch.

## Services & pricing

- Diagnostic visit: $89, waived if you proceed with the repair same visit.
- Common repairs (capacitor, ignitor, blower motor, refrigerant leak): $150–$1,200 flat-rate quoted upfront.
- Furnace replacement (80%–96% AFUE gas): $4,500–$9,500 installed.
- Central AC replacement (14.3–18 SEER2): $4,800–$11,000 installed.
- Heat pump system (ducted, cold-climate rated): $8,000–$16,000 installed; qualifies for federal 25C tax credit up to $2,000 and Xcel Energy rebates.
- Ductless mini-split: $3,500–$5,500 per zone installed.
- Seasonal tune-up (heating or cooling): $129; included free for Summit Care Club members.

## Process & timeline

Repairs: technician diagnoses, quotes flat-rate, most repairs completed same visit (trucks stock common parts). Replacements: free 60–90 minute in-home estimate with load calculation (Manual J — we size the system, we never guess from the old unit's label), written quote with good/better/best options, install typically 1–2 days, city permit and inspection included in price.

## FAQs

**How long does a furnace last?** 15–20 years typical in Colorado. Past 15 years with a repair over $1,000, we'll show you the repair-vs-replace math and let you decide.

**Do you install equipment bought elsewhere?** No. We warranty labor and equipment together; we can't stand behind equipment we didn't source.

**Why is my upstairs always hot?** Usually duct balance or undersized returns, not the equipment. We check ductwork as part of any estimate; sometimes a $600 duct fix beats a $10,000 system.

**Is a heat pump enough for Denver winters?** Modern cold-climate heat pumps run efficiently to -13°F. Most homes pair one with a gas furnace backup (dual fuel), which our estimators price both ways.
