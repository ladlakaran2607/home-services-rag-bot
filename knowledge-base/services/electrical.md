Repairs and single-circuit work: same visit. Panel upgrades: 1 day on site plus utility scheduling (typically 1–2 weeks lead time — Xcel controls that timeline, not us). Rewires: 3–7 days, done in stages so you keep power each night, drywall patching included.

## FAQs

**My breaker keeps tripping — is that dangerous?** A breaker doing its job isn't dangerous, but repeated trips mean overload, a failing breaker, or a fault. Don't upsize the breaker; that's how fires start. Diagnose first.

**Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?** Not always. A load calculation tells us. If your panel is full, a load-management device ($400–$700) is often cheaper than a full upgrade.

**Are Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels really a problem?** Yes — both have documented failure-to-trip issues and most insurers surcharge or decline them. Replacement is the standard recommendation.

**Aluminum wiring — replace everything?** Not necessarily. COPALUM/AlumiConn remediation at connection points is a code-accepted alternative to full rewire at roughly a third of the cost.
