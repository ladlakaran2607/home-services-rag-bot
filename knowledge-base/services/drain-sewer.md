## FAQs

**Multiple drains backing up at once — what does that mean?** That's a main line problem, not a fixture clog. Stop running water and call; if sewage is surfacing it's an emergency dispatch.

**Do tree roots mean my whole line is bad?** Not necessarily. Minor intrusion in clay-pipe joints can be managed for years with annual jetting. Collapsed or badly offset pipe can't. The camera footage decides, and you see it yourself.

**Is trenchless always better?** When it's viable, usually — but badly collapsed lines or bellies (sags) can rule it out. We quote trenched and trenchless side by side when both work.

**Why did my clog come back a month later?** A cable opens a hole through grease; it doesn't clean the pipe wall. Recurring kitchen-line clogs usually want jetting, which scours to the pipe wall.
