# Backup Generators

## Overview

Whole-home standby generators (Generac, Kohler) and portable-generator interlock setups. Standby units start automatically within seconds of an outage and run on natural gas or propane. Installation is genuinely cross-trade — gas line, electrical transfer switch, concrete pad — and our crews handle all of it under one permit set.

## Process & timeline

Assessment sizes the unit against your panel loads (what must stay on: furnace, well pump, medical equipment, fridge, office). Quote covers unit, pad, gas line, transfer switch, permits, and startup. Lead time 2–6 weeks for unit availability; install is 1–2 days plus utility/inspection scheduling.

## FAQs

**Standby or battery (solar) backup?** Batteries are silent, clean, and cover short outages; standby generators run indefinitely on gas and cost less per kW for whole-home coverage. Long outages or well pumps favor the generator; pairing with solar is possible — see the solar service line and we'll model both.

**What size do I need?** A 22 kW covers most 2,500–3,500 sq ft homes fully; essential-circuits packages cover less for less money. The load calculation decides, not the brochure.

**Does it need maintenance if it never runs?** Yes — it self-tests weekly and still needs annual service. A generator that isn't maintained is the one that fails during the storm you bought it for.

**Do I need a bigger gas meter?** Sometimes. Standby units draw significant gas; we check meter capacity during assessment and coordinate an upgrade with Xcel if required (their timeline, typically 2–4 weeks).
