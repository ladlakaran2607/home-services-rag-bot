# Windows & Doors

## Overview

Replacement windows, entry doors, patio doors, and egress windows. Vinyl, fiberglass, and wood-clad lines from Pella, Andersen, and Alside, installed by in-house carpenters. Energy-efficiency focus: dual/triple pane, low-E coatings rated for Colorado's sun and temperature swings.

## Services & pricing

- Free in-home consultation and measurement.
- Vinyl replacement window (dual-pane, low-E, argon): $450–$800 per window installed.
- Fiberglass or wood-clad window: $800–$1,200 per window installed.
- Full-home window replacement (15–20 windows): $8,000–$20,000 typical.
- Entry door (fiberglass or steel, installed): $1,800–$4,500.
- Sliding or French patio door: $2,500–$6,000 installed.
- Egress window with well excavation (basement code compliance): $3,500–$6,500.

## Process & timeline

Consultation → exact measurement visit → order (manufacturing lead 3–6 weeks) → install day (most full homes done in 1–2 days) → city inspection where required. Old windows and doors hauled away; openings insulated and sealed as part of every install.

## FAQs

**How much will new windows save on energy?** Typically 10–20% on heating/cooling for single-pane replacements; less if you already have decent dual-pane. We'll say plainly if your money is better spent on insulation first (see insulation service line).

**Do I need permits?** Like-for-like replacement usually doesn't; egress windows and size changes do. We pull any required permits.

**Full-frame or pocket replacement?** Pocket (insert) is cheaper and faster if frames are sound; full-frame is right when there's rot or you want more glass area. We quote whichever fits, and explain why.

**Condensation between panes — repair or replace?** A failed seal means the insulated glass unit is dead. Sometimes just the glass unit can be swapped ($250–$450) rather than the whole window; we check before quoting a full replacement.

## FAQs — egress

**When is an egress window legally required?** Any basement bedroom needs one (Colorado follows IRC): minimum opening 5.7 sq ft, sill within 44" of floor. Required for legal bedroom status in a sale or rental.
