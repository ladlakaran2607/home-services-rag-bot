# Summit Home Services — Company Overview

Summit Home Services is a multi-trade home services company serving the Denver metro area since 2009. One phone number, twelve trades, in-house crews (no subcontracting on core trades). Licensed, bonded, and insured in Colorado (License #HS-2009-1147).

## Service area

Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Thornton, Centennial, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Broomfield, Golden, and Parker. Jobs outside this area may carry a $49 trip fee; confirmed at booking.

## Hours and emergency service

- Standard hours: Mon–Sat, 7am–7pm.
- 24/7 emergency dispatch for plumbing, HVAC (no-heat/no-cool), and electrical hazards. Emergency dispatch fee $129, credited toward the repair if you proceed.
- Non-emergency trades (roofing, solar, windows, insulation, gutters, smart home) book 2–10 business days out depending on season.

## Pricing philosophy

Flat-rate, upfront pricing. A technician diagnoses, quotes a fixed price before work starts, and the price does not change unless the scope changes and you approve it in writing. Diagnostic/service-call fee is $89 for most trades, waived if you proceed with the repair the same visit.

## Financing — Summit FlexPay

- 0% interest for 12 months on jobs over $1,000 (on approved credit).
- Term loans up to 120 months on jobs over $5,000 (typical APR 7.9%–13.9%).
- Applies to installs and replacements in every trade; not available for diagnostic fees or memberships.

## Summit Care Club (membership)

$19/month per home. Includes: annual HVAC tune-up (heating + cooling), annual plumbing inspection, annual electrical safety check, 15% off all repairs, priority scheduling, waived diagnostic fees, and doubled labor warranties.

## Guarantees and warranties

- Workmanship warranty: 2 years on repairs, 5 years on installs (10 years roofing workmanship). Doubled for Care Club members on repairs.
- Manufacturer warranties pass through on all installed equipment; we register products on your behalf.
- 100% satisfaction guarantee: if a repair fails within the workmanship window, we return and fix it free.

## Booking and what to expect

Book by phone, web chat, or online form. You get a 2-hour arrival window, a text with the technician's name and photo when they're en route, and a written quote by email before any work begins. Estimates for replacements/installs (HVAC, roofing, solar, windows, water heaters, generators) are free and take 60–90 minutes on site.

## Multi-trade bundles

Because crews are in-house, cross-trade projects are coordinated by one project manager: common bundles are roof + solar, roof + gutters, HVAC + insulation + smart thermostat, and water heater + drain inspection. Bundles receive a 5% multi-trade discount when two or more trades are on one signed proposal.
